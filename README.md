<h2>Building software businesses, not just software</h2>

<p>
I’m Leon Spors — a software entrepreneur focused on designing and operating
production-grade systems.
I build products end-to-end: from architecture and infrastructure to execution,
operations, and long-term maintenance.
</p>

<p>
My work sits at the intersection of backend engineering, distributed systems,
and product infrastructure.
I’m particularly interested in reliability, observability, and performance —
not as buzzwords, but as properties that determine whether a system can be trusted
when it actually matters.
</p>

<p>
I spend a lot of time thinking about how systems behave under stress:
partial outages, degraded performance, inconsistent data, and operational blind spots.
Most of the products I build are born out of firsthand frustration with existing tools
that look good on paper but fail in real-world conditions.
</p>

<p>
As an entrepreneur, I value ownership and long-term thinking.
I prefer building fewer things with higher leverage, focusing on clarity of design,
measurable behavior, and predictable scaling.
That often means making deliberate trade-offs instead of chasing trends.
</p>

<p>
I’m hands-on across the entire stack and comfortable operating what I build.
From backend services and data pipelines to deployment, monitoring, and iteration,
I believe products should be engineered with the assumption that
they will be used, stressed, and questioned.
</p>

<p>
You can find my ongoing work, experiments, and products at
<a href="https://spors.io">spors.io</a>.
</p>
